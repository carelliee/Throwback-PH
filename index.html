Every Era Has a Story

The 1990s was a memorable period in Philippine popular culture. It was a time of iconic OPM songs, colorful fashion trends, handwritten letters, cassette tapes, and shared experiences that brought people together.

Throwback PH takes visitors on a journey through these cultural moments, showing how everyday life in the past compares with the present and what the future may bring.

A Changing Philippines

Then & Now

Compare important events and cultural developments from the past with the present.

Social

1991: Mount Pinatubo
2020: COVID-19

Both events affected communities and changed everyday life. The Mount Pinatubo eruption displaced thousands, while COVID-19 changed social interaction, education, and public health.

Economic

1997: Asian Financial Crisis
Today: Inflation

Economic challenges affect the cost of living, employment, and household spending. The Asian Financial Crisis affected the region, while inflation remains a concern for Filipino families.

Political

1991: Senate Vote on U.S. Bases
2022: National Elections

Political participation and national decisions shape the country's direction. The 1991 Senate vote concerned the U.S. military bases agreement, while the 2022 elections reflected the choices of Filipino voters.

Cultural

1990s: Filipino Rock & OPM
Today: P-Pop

Filipino music continues to evolve. The 1990s popularized bands and alternative rock, while today's P-Pop groups combine Filipino identity with modern global influences.

Environmental

1992: Rio Earth Summit
Today: El Niño

Environmental concerns highlight the importance of protecting natural resources. The Rio Earth Summit encouraged global cooperation, while El Niño brings drought and water challenges.

Others: Technology & Innovation

1990s: Early Internet
Today: Artificial Intelligence

Internet access opened new ways to communicate and find information. Today, artificial intelligence is changing how people learn, work, and create.

Looking Ahead

Philippines 2030

What might Philippine popular culture and everyday life look like in the next few years?

Body & Self-Image

Fashion may become more personalized through AI styling, digital fashion tools, and wearable technology.

Technology

AI-powered tools, smart devices, and digital payments may become even more common in everyday life.

Feelings & Relationships

Digital communication may continue to shape friendships, relationships, entertainment, and self-expression.

Social & Political Life

Online platforms may create more opportunities for civic participation, awareness, and public discussion.

Culture

Filipino music, films, and creative works may reach wider audiences through global digital platforms.

Environment

Sustainability, renewable energy, and climate awareness may become bigger parts of Filipino communities.
